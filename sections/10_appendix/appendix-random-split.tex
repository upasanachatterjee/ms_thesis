\section{Full Donald Trump Mediation Table (Random Split)}

Table~\ref{tab:random-te-trump-full} reports Total Effect, Natural Direct Effect, and Natural Indirect Effect estimates for Donald Trump sentiment as treatment with Politics sentiment as mediator under the AllSidesV2 random split ($N = 572$). Both the $Q_{25} \to Q_{75}$ and $P_{10} \to P_{90}$ percentile contrasts are reported; under the random split these correspond to the treatment windows $-0.6 \to 0.0$ and $-1.1 \to +0.3$ respectively (compared to $-0.4 \to +0.2$ and $-0.9 \to +0.5$ under the media split).

Neither paradigm reaches significance on the Total Effect under either percentile contrast, and both paradigms assign the dominant share of the Total Effect to the direct channel. The Fine-tuned RoBERTa Natural Indirect Effect is negative under both contrasts on the random split ($-0.017$ on $Q_{25} \to Q_{75}$, $-0.054$ on $P_{10} \to P_{90}$), whereas under the media split the corresponding RoBERTa NIEs were positive ($+0.019$ and $+0.040$; Table~\ref{tab:te-trump}). Human NIEs are small and positive in both splits under the $Q_{25} \to Q_{75}$ contrast but differ in sign under $P_{10} \to P_{90}$ ($+0.014$ media, $-0.012$ random). No NIE reaches 95\% significance in any paradigm-contrast-split combination.
